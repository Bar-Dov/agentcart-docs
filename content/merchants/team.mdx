# Team management

Each AgentCart account belongs to an **organization**. You can invite teammates to share access to all your stores, scans, and analytics.

## Where to manage your team

Open the **Org** tab in the sidebar of [app.agentcart.io](https://app.agentcart.io). You'll see:

- Your organization's name (owners can rename it inline)
- The list of current members and their roles
- Pending invitations
- A button to send a new invitation (owners only)

## Roles

| Role | Can do |
|------|--------|
| **Owner** | Everything members can do, plus: invite teammates, revoke invitations, remove members, rename the organization |
| **Member** | Manage stores, run scans, view analytics, configure capabilities |

Every organization keeps at least one owner. If you're the only owner, you can't be removed and you can't leave — invite another owner first.

> More granular roles (admin / viewer / billing) are on the roadmap. The current matrix is the v1 baseline.

## Inviting a teammate

1. On the **Org** page, click **+ Invite** (top right of the Team members card).
2. Enter their email and pick a role.
3. They'll receive an email titled **"You've been invited to AgentCart"** with an accept link.
4. The link is valid for **14 days** and is single-use.

When they click the link:
- If they already have an AgentCart account with that email, they're added to your org and redirected to the dashboard.
- If they don't, they're asked to sign up first, then automatically joined to your org.

The invitation appears under **Pending invitations** on the Org page until it's accepted or expires. Owners can revoke a pending invitation at any time.

## Limits

To discourage abuse, invitations are rate-limited:

- Up to **10 active (unaccepted) invitations** per organization
- Up to **20 invitations per owner per 24 hours**

Revoke a pending invitation to free up a slot.

## Removing a member

Owners can remove any member except the last remaining owner. Click the **⋯** icon next to their row and confirm. Removed members instantly lose access to all stores in the organization.

## Leaving an organization

Members can leave at any time via **Leave organization** at the bottom of the Team members list. Owners can only leave after promoting another member to owner.
